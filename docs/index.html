Klickitat Parcels

Shows Klickitat County parcels and where you are, colored by land status:

  • Public land, or a parcel you marked Allowed
  • Within 50 ft (plus GPS error) of an off-limits boundary
  • Inside private or other off-limits land
  • Unknown: weak GPS or unclassified parcel

Navigation aid only. County parcel lines are not surveys and can be off by 20–100+ ft; phone GPS adds its own error. Posted signs, fences, and survey markers take precedence.

Alerts only work while the app is open on screen. iPhone doesn't let web apps track you with the screen off.
